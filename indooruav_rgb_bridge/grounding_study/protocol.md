# Paired evaluation protocol (fixed before running the arms)

All four arms use checkpoint 29999, seed 42 + decision index, local calibrated RGB, fixed spawn gimbal and the original instruction. The policy has no expert files, episode IDs, global goals, map, projected landmarks, oracle future poses or fitted weights. Direction labels and any reference-derived evaluation ranges are scoring-only. No local training. The two executor budgets are 60 decisions / 60 simulated seconds, with the usual 2 s STOP hold on flight. Direct placement continues after an outside endpoint. Physical motion uses the original 20 Hz controller and safety termination.

**RGB.** Six historical visually checked object–image positives, paired seeds 16/42/77; historical baseline and correct-hint outputs can be reused after exact verification. Ten negative object–view pairs: all three queried objects in each of two corridor starts and the search-room start, laptop in the AC east-to-west start. Score a left/right hint on absence once per unique image–target pair, and separately score a >2° actual turn over three seeds. Gold abstains on absence; baseline supplies no explicit hint. DINO tiny frozen, single phrase per target, text threshold 0.25, detection threshold 0.35, horizontal central band 45–55%, short side 640, maximum 960. For a centered detection, provide a centered hint. On abstention preserve the original text. No score/threshold selection on this panel.

**Control.** Evaluate baseline, RGB hint, task-conditioned waypoint check, and both on both executors. Hint target sequence follows the text; for an explicit laptop-then-sofa search, switch after two consecutive centered laptop detections of at least 3% image area. The guard uses the instruction, odometry and proposed waypoint only. It bounds local motion, keeps explicitly requested cruise altitude, and applies phase-dependent rise/slow-down rules; when tasked to search, it limits translation before a scan. It incorporates no expert action or pose sequence. Log raw and executed candidates including a raw STOP that leaves a stage incomplete.

**Cases.** Six previous starts plus four fresh starts, from room_inspect_ac, room_search_laptop_sofa, corridor_high_return, corridor_climb_accelerate. Offsets in spawn forward/left metres and yaw degrees respectively (+0.12,+0.08,+12), (-0.12,+0.08,-12), (+0.15,-0.10,+12), (-0.15,+0.10,-12). Same instructions and same nominal task goals. Verify spawn safety and distinctness from all recorded starts; if unsafe, halve translation, up to twice, without policy rollouts. Freeze policy before inspecting novel rollout results. Results for familiar starts and fresh starts reported separately. Fresh starts test same-scene transfer only.

**Scores.** Flight strict requires a STOP with stable hold (2 s, drift ≤0.12 m, last 0.5 s speed ≤0.10 m/s), nominal endpoint ≤0.12 m, yaw ≤8°, no collision/outside, and instruction evidence. Inspect/ordered center-and-range use previous offline reference thresholds; arc tasks additionally require ≥20° target-relative azimuth span while centered after approach and target centered on stopping. Cruise requires absolute height error ≤0.20 m throughout. Climb/descend requires ≥0.12 m climb then ≥0.12 m descent, plus horizontal speed increase ≥0.15 m/s followed by decrease ≥0.15 m/s after peak. Report these components, first rule violation in raw vs executed requests, altitude MAE, position-only SR ≤2 m and NE to the expert's measured end (held-out pose change keeps the same goal), and counts of episodes with collision/outside. Direct has no physical strict SR, speed dynamics or swept collisions; report endpoint, discrete profile, ever-outside and semantic scores separately. A rule violation is an operational proxy; a missing task subgoal at STOP is flagged separately.

**User-requested egocentric bearing amendment, prior to running policy arms.** Retain the matched image-side probe; additionally score body front/back (cosine of azimuth), 8-way horizontal bearing (45° sectors), abstention/coverage, and progress toward the target by absolute angular error. On a matched stationary viewpoint, rotate the camera with the body by 0°, ±90°, and 180°. Compare current-only with a causal buffer of RGB detections and yaw (no query-time panorama in flight), gold used only in the offline arm. Near a rear target either initial turn direction can reduce error. Replay RGB only; changing yaw is a controlled diagnostic. Physical hints use calibrated pixel rays, timestamped/yaw-compensated object bearings, forget on displacement >0.35 m or age >8 decisions. Absence with no evidence produces no directional hint. Distinguish being behind from moving backward. Report visibility and absence separately.
